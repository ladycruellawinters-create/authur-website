ONE BOOK. SEVERAL OPINIONS.

An evening with the book club.

Evelyn Greene organises the town’s monthly book club, with Daniel hosting at Twilight Books after sunset. Expect lively conversation, enthusiastic opinions and a very firm interest in whether everyone has finished the book.

A small request from Evelyn

“Please allow everyone to take their seats before announcing that you guessed the ending. Some of us would like the chance to be surprised.”
