A TASTE OF CEDAR HOLLOW

Your usual booth awaits.

Pull up a seat at The Olive and Adder, where American diner comforts meet Greek favourites.

CEDAR HOLLOW, MAINE

The Olive and Adder

Ἡ Ἐλαία καὶ ἡ Ἔχιδνα

Good food. Warm coffee. Room for one more.

Breakfast, wheneverἈκράτισμα

  • Butter & syrup pancakes

    Πλακοῦντες μετὰ βουτύρου καὶ μέλιτος

    A golden stack with melting butter and sweet syrup.

  • The diner breakfast

    Ὠὰ καὶ ὕειον κρέας

    Eggs your way, crisp bacon, home fries and toast.

  • Spinach & feta omelette

    Ὠὰ μετὰ λαχάνων καὶ τυροῦ

    Folded eggs with spinach, feta and a little onion.

  • Honey yoghurt bowl

    Ὀξύγαλα μετὰ μέλιτος

    Thick Greek yoghurt, honey, walnuts and seasonal fruit.

From the grillἈπὸ τῆς ἐσχάρας

  • The usual cheeseburger

    Βοὸς κρέας μετὰ τυροῦ

    A beef patty, melting cheese, lettuce, tomato and pickles. Golden fries alongside.

  • Grilled cheese & tomato soup

    Ἄρτος καὶ τυρός, μετὰ ζωμοῦ

    Toasted bread, plenty of cheese and a comforting cup of tomato soup.

  • Chicken souvlaki

    Ὀβελίσκοι ὀρνίθειοι

    Lemon-and-oregano chicken skewers, warm pita, tzatziki and salad.

  • Lamb gyro pita

    Ἄρνειον κρέας ἐν ἄρτῳ

    Seasoned lamb, tomato, red onion and tzatziki tucked into warm pita.

A little taste of GreeceἙλληνικὰ ἐδέσματα

  • Moussaka

    Λαχάνων καὶ κρέως πλακοῦς

    Layers of aubergine, seasoned beef and potatoes beneath a creamy béchamel topping.

  • Spanakopita

    Λαχάνων καὶ τυροῦ πλακοῦς

    Crisp filo filled with spinach, feta and herbs, served with a side salad.

  • Village salad

    Λάχανα μετὰ τυροῦ καὶ ἐλαιῶν

    Tomato, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta, dressed with olive oil and oregano.

  • Lemon potatoes

    Ῥίζαι μετὰ κιτρίου

    Golden roast potatoes with lemon, olive oil and oregano. Lovely on the side.

Under the cake standsΠέμματα καὶ πλακοῦντες

  • Baklava

    Πλακοῦς μετὰ καρύων καὶ μέλιτος

    Delicate layers of filo, chopped nuts and fragrant honey syrup.

  • Pie of the day

    Ὁ τῆς ἡμέρας πλακοῦς

    Ask what is beneath the glass today. Add a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

  • Cinnamon rolls & muffins

    Πέμματα γλυκέα

    Something soft, sweet and very difficult to walk past.

  • Chocolate celebration cake

    Πλακοῦς ἑορτάσιμος

    A generous slice. No special occasion required.

  • Rock cakes

    Πέμματα πετρώδη

    A crumbly little fruit cake to enjoy with your coffee.

Stay for another cupΠοτά

  • House coffee

    Πόμα θερμόν

    Rich, dark and best enjoyed without watching the clock.

  • Tea & honey

    Πόμα μετὰ μέλιτος

    A warming pot, with honey on the side.

  • Classic milkshakes

    Γάλα γλυκύ

    Vanilla, chocolate or strawberry, served cold in a tall glass.
