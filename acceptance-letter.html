Mynydd Draig

Ysbryd y ddraig, calon y mynydd

Spirit of the dragon, heart of the mountain

Rhuddlan Mountain

Dear future student,

It is an honour to inform you that you have been offered a place at Mynydd Draig. If you choose to accept, please send your correspondence at your earliest convenience to Headmistress Imelda Penrose, Headmistress’s Office, Mynydd Draig, Rhuddlan Mountain.

Term will begin on the first Monday of September, but you will be expected to arrive by homing key on the final Friday of August.

Any belongings you wish to bring with you should be packed into a personal trunk, and any pets must be safely secured in a housing cage before your arrival.

We look forward to hearing from you soon.

May the mountain guide your path,
Headmistress Imelda Penrose.
