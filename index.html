MYNYDD DRAIG · UNPUBLISHED

Mynydd Draig: What Lies Beyond the Yew

Rooted in Wales and set among its mountains, Mynydd Draig follows Owain Ifans, an orphan who discovers a hidden world of magic. At a magical school nestled in the mountains, he makes friends and finds a place to call home. But as mysteries unfold, Owain must confront threats to the people—and the world—he has come to love.

MY NEIGHBOUR IS · UNPUBLISHED

My Neighbour Is a Vampire

When young Oliver Hart inherits his great-aunt Agnes’s house, he packs up his life and moves to the idyllic little town of Cedar Hollow. At first, it seems perfectly ordinary—until he discovers that his neighbours are vampires, werewolves, and witches. Follow Oliver as he navigates the town’s mysteries in a series that’s cosy, heartwarming, and delightfully camp.
