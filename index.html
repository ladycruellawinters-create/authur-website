01 / BEHIND THE WORDS✧

A little about
the author.

I’m C. T. Winters, author of Mynydd Draig and My neighbour is.

I was born in Colwyn Bay on the north coast of Wales. Although my family moved to England when I was young, Wales has always called me back—and its mountains, stories, and magic have found their way into my writing.

My first book began with a writing prompt from my English teacher in high school. What started as an assignment became a whole novel. That story has since been lost to time, though I’m tempted to see how much I can bring back from memory.

Growing up with Merlin, Teen Wolf, and Murder, She Wrote, I suppose magical schools, supernatural neighbours, and small-town mysteries were always going to appear somewhere.

Away from writing, I enjoy decorating and I am gradually turning my house into a haunted Victorian manor, shared with six cats of assorted shapes, sizes, and colours. I’m eccentric, prone to taking the complicated route, and capable of becoming completely absorbed in a project I love.

Above all, I want readers to find a place to call home in my stories. That’s what I looked for in books as a child. Giving someone else that feeling would mean more to me than anything.

C. T. Winters

MYNYDD DRAIG · UNPUBLISHED

Mynydd Draig: What Lies Beyond the Yew

For eleven-year-old Owain Ifans, being overlooked is nothing new. Neither are the nightmares of an ancient forest—and something waiting among its trees.

Then a red kite delivers a letter, opening the way to Mynydd Draig, a magical school hidden in the Welsh mountains. Between enchanted portraits, unpredictable lessons, and new friends Albion and Thatcher, Owain begins to discover something he’s never truly had: a place to belong.

But a new home doesn’t mean an escape from his dreams. As unsettling warnings and gaps in the school’s history draw him deeper into its mysteries, Owain must decide whom to trust—and how far he’ll go to protect the people who have made him feel at home.

A Welsh-rooted fantasy of friendship, belonging, and secrets that refuse to stay buried.

MY NEIGHBOUR IS · UNPUBLISHED

My Neighbour Is a Vampire

Oliver Hart arrives in Cedar Hollow with a simple plan: fix up his late great-aunt Agnes’s house, sell it, and move on. But between the town’s enthusiastic welcome and a charming café worker named Noah, leaving starts to feel less straightforward.

Then there’s Daniel Blackwood, the neighbour across the street. His unusual hours and curious habits give Oliver plenty to wonder about—and in Cedar Hollow, a perfectly reasonable explanation can be surprisingly hard to find.

A cosy, heartwarming mystery with a budding romance, a generous helping of camp, and a fresh start that refuses to go quite to plan.
